Privacy Policy

App: zisheng7777 Sheets MCP

Effective date: 2026-03-23

What data is accessed

With your explicit Google OAuth consent, this app may access Google Sheets and related Google Drive file metadata/content needed to read or update spreadsheets you authorize.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform spreadsheet read/write actions requested by you in your local Cursor workflow. The app does not sell user data.

Storage

OAuth tokens may be stored locally by the Cursor / MCP client on your machine for re-authentication. Spreadsheet content is not independently republished by this app.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with third parties, except as required to operate Google API access under your authorization.
